# Last Acre — Kickstarter Campaign Design

**Date:** 2026-05-05
**Status:** Approved by user, ready for implementation planning

---

## Overview

A Kickstarter campaign to fund development equipment (hardware + software) needed to finish Last Acre's base game and unlock the path to multiplayer and 3D. Solo developer, zero existing audience, 1–2 month launch timeline.

**Funding goal:** $6,000 (covers equipment, taxes, Kickstarter fees, and payment processing)
**Max pledge tier:** $20
**Backers needed to fund:** ~300+

---

## Campaign Context

- **Game:** Last Acre — a full farming tycoon with a living economy, neighbours mechanic, animal genetics, workers, auctions, and more
- **Developer:** Solo dev
- **Current state:** Screenshots and gameplay video exist; no public playable build
- **Key differentiator:** The neighbours mechanic (neighbours compete for your land in real time) — no other farming sim has this
- **Visual identity:** Dark cinematic aesthetic from the Last Game website — near-black (`#0A0C0F`), golden amber (`#E8A935`), cream text (`#F5EDD8`), heavy Playfair Display serif headings, grain texture overlay
- **Existing asset:** Last Game website at `C:\Users\SanGi\.antigravity\FArM TYCOON\LAST-ACRE-CORP` — all campaign materials must match this visual style

## Campaign Page Structure

All campaign visuals, copy tone, and layout must match the Last Game website aesthetic.

### 1. Banner
- Game title ("LAST ACRE") in heavy serif, large
- Tagline: "Your land. Your legacy."
- "Funding now on Kickstarter" badge with amber pulse dot
- Key art or in-game screenshot (no video hero)
- Atmospheric earth-tone gradient + grain texture

### 2. The Hook
- Eyebrow: "The Game"
- Headline: "One developer. One farming sim unlike any other."
- Body: Introduce the game, lead with the neighbours mechanic as the differentiator, establish that one person built the whole thing

### 3. What Your Pledge Funds
- Eyebrow: "What Your Pledge Funds"
- Headline: "Exactly where the money goes."
- Itemized budget table — transparent, no ambiguity:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Development PC upgrade (GPU + RAM) | $3,200 |
| 3D & engine software licenses | $1,100 |
| Sound design & music licensing | $800 |
| QA, playtesting & misc tools | $500 |
| Kickstarter fees & payment processing | $400 |
| **Total** | **$6,000** |

### 4. The Roadmap
Three steps, linear progression:
1. **Base game completion (this campaign)** — sound, onboarding, final polish across all systems
2. **Multiplayer** — compete against real players in the neighbours mechanic, real-time land auctions
3. **3D mode** — full 3D farm view

### 5. Reward Tiers
Every backer at any tier receives a **full digital copy of Last Acre** when it ships. This is called out prominently above the tiers.

| Tier | Price | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| Seedling | $5 | Digital copy + name in credits + social shoutout |
| Farmer | $10 | Digital copy + early access at launch (before public release) + credits + monthly dev update |
| Neighbour | $20 | Digital copy + early beta access (before game is feature-complete) + name as in-game neighbour character + credits + monthly dev update |

### 6. About the Developer
- Brief personal section with photo/avatar
- Solo dev story: built every system from scratch, passion project, clear vision
- Human, direct tone — not a pitch, a person

## Pre-Launch Marketing Plan (Weeks 1–4)

### Track 1 — Personal Network
- Compile a personal list of everyone to contact directly (friends, family, colleagues)
- Send personal DMs/messages at launch — not mass blasts, individual asks
- Target: first 20–50 backers, seeds early momentum for Kickstarter's algorithm

### Track 2 — Community Seeding
Start 2–3 weeks before launch. Tone: share genuinely, don't lead with the campaign.
- Reddit: r/indiegaming, r/AndroidGaming, r/iosgaming, farming sim subreddits
- Discord: indie game servers, farming game communities
- itch.io forums
- Angle: devlogs, show the neighbours mechanic, ask for feedback — build goodwill before mentioning Kickstarter

### Track 3 — Small Creator Outreach
- Identify 8–10 TikTokers/YouTubers (1k–50k subs) covering indie games or farming sims
- Send short personal pitches with gameplay screenshots and early access offer
- Target: 2–3 covering the campaign at or around launch day
- Start outreach in week 2 — gives time for responses and scheduling

### Email Signup (Immediate)
- Upgrade the existing `DownloadCta` section on the Last Game website into a proper email signup form
- Replace the current mailto "Get Notified" button with an email input + submit
- Messaging changes from "Coming Soon" to "Coming to Kickstarter"
- Section sits between Trailer and StudioAbout — no page restructuring needed
- Every pre-launch signup = guaranteed launch-day notification
- This is the highest-ROI action before the campaign is live

### Launch Day
- Post simultaneously across all seeded communities
- Send personal DMs to network
- Activate any creator coverage timed to launch

### During Campaign
- Weekly backer updates
- Progress posts at 50% funded and final 48 hours
- Re-engage communities at those same milestones

## Success Criteria

- Campaign fully funds ($6,000) within 30-day window
- Neighbour tier ($20) has strong uptake — these backers become in-game characters, high engagement incentive
- Email list built before launch to seed day-one momentum
- Campaign page visually consistent with Last Game website

## Out of Scope

- Physical reward tiers
- Stretch goals (keep it simple for a first campaign)
- Paid advertising
- Tier pricing above $20
